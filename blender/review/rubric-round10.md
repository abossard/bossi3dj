# Synthwave Planet, review round 10 rubric

Frozen before the first grab of round 10. It does not change during the round. File names as in round 9 (`CAM_<n>_<day|sunset|night>_...`, orbit shots `S9_*`, night pair tag `grid0` / `grid1`, flyover pair tag `fly_a` / `fly_b`). A shot passes when every line that applies holds; a failed line names its id and where in the frame. Counterexample of what must never appear: the reference image given with the call.

| ID | Line |
|---|---|
| R10-DAY | By day, no lines on land or water, and the land still reads through shading and haze. |
| R10-LATTICE | No regular lattice or grid lines anywhere, on the ground or from orbit (counterexample: the reference image); any line ends at a vertex of the drawn mesh. |
| R10-ATTACHED | Lines do not move against the terrain. |

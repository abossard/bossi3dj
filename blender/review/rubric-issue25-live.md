# Rubric: issue #25 live, the rebuilt planet (2026-11-17)

Frozen before the review. Pairs from the demo program (Magic drives the music values, the controls held) at the same program seconds: `before_<t>.png` (the keep, fog by height, sky framing and the haze's rise switched off; the sky still unhued, which is built in) and `after_<t>.png` (as built). t 9, 12, 28, 34 are blends 2 to 9 km up; t 45 and 60 low shots; t 75 a vantage on the moon; t 125 the planet from space (64 km).

- **L25-LAND**: in the `after` blends (t 9, 12, 28, 34) land with readable shapes fills part of the frame, no flat colour field, and the horizon or the limb shows (not land to every edge). Pass when every one does.
- **L25-SKY**: in `after` the sky, the sun, the moon and the limb's glow keep their own colours (pink, violet, blue and orange of the synthwave sky), not turned green or teal; the land may carry the hue. Pass when no sky reads green or teal.
- **L25-MOON**: at t 75 `after` shows the moon and land in one frame. Pass when both show.
- **L25-SPACE**: at t 125 `after` shows the planet's disc with its limb; no pale band or halo much wider than a thin glow. Pass when so.
- **L25-LOW**: at t 45 and 60 `after` looks as `before`, apart from the camera's own small differences. Pass when no difference stands out.

# Synthwave Planet, review round 13 rubric

Frozen before the first change of round 13 (2026-11-12). It does not change during the round. Grabs are named `<cam>_<day|sunset|night>_f<fog>_e<eclipse>[_p<phase>][_<tag>]` as in earlier rounds; tags: `neb0` (`/vj/nebula` 0), `limb` (the 200 mm orbit limb proof shot). A shot passes when every line that applies holds; a failed line names its id and where in the frame.

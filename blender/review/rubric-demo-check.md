# Rubric: the demo check (tools/live_demo_check.sh)

The planet's unheld output as a viewer gets it, from the 90 s recording. One contact sheet, 4 x 3 tiles of 480 px, one tile every 4.5 s, left to right, top to bottom. Frozen before the review.

- **D-PICTURE**: every tile shows a finished scene: land, sky and the pyramids or the camera's target, no black, grey or half-drawn tile.
- **D-GROUND**: no hole, missing triangle, pale ring or floating pyramid on the land, the ridges or the pyramids' foundations.
- **D-FLOW**: neighbouring tiles read as one moving flight (the camera moves on, the light changes gradually), not as unrelated stills or a frozen frame.
- **D-LOOK**: the synthwave look holds: readable colours, no clipped white areas, no strobe-like flash.

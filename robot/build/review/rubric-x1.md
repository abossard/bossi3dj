# Robot Exchanges preview, round X1 (frozen 2026-11-17)

Shots: one contact sheet per Exchange from the Eevee preview (960x540; Eevee draws the Shadow Catcher as a plain floor, so the floor shows). Row 1 is `duel_wide` (a profile from the side, both full figures), row 2 `duel_low` (low behind the Android, looking up at the giant). Tiles in order: the first frame, each Hit frame (up to 4), the last frame; labels end in the frame number. Judge staging, contact and readability, not materials or light.

The Android is the small white humanoid robot (about 1.9 m); the giant is the Security Bot scaled to 6 m, standing about 2.5 m in front of it, facing it. The sheet name is the Exchange; what happens in it is in robot/build/GIANT.md (table "Moves: Exchanges").

| ID | Pass when | Counterexample (fail) |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | Both fighters are in frame in every tile: duel_wide shows both full figures head to feet; duel_low shows the Android in the foreground and the giant from feet to head behind it. | A head, hammer arm or foot cut by the frame edge; the Android hidden behind the giant or out of frame; the camera inside a fighter. |
| X2 | The giant reads as a giant: much taller and heavier than the Android, standing on the floor. | The two read as the same size; the giant floating or sunk into the floor. |
| X3 | Every Hit tile shows its contact as named in GIANT.md: the hammer on the Android's head or the floor, the Android's fist on the giant's knee or chest core, a foot stomping the floor. | A strike that visibly misses its target, or a Hit tile where nothing touches. |
| X4 | No limb of either fighter passes through the other fighter or through the floor. | The hammer inside the Android's torso, the Android's arm inside the giant's leg, a foot below the floor. |
| X5 | Each fighter's pose reads as deliberate and balanced; the giant faces the Android. | A tangled or broken rig, the giant turned away, a limb bent backwards. |

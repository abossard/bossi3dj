# Rubric: Geo-Scatter biome previews behind the Robot arena (round B1)

One frame per biome, front Shot Camera, Eevee low, half size. The Android (or the M2000) stands in the foreground on a dark floor; the biome is scattered on a 200 x 230 m plane behind it (+Y, 15 m to 245 m away).

Per shot, judge:
1. city_visible: is the scattered biome visible at all behind the figure? (yes / barely / no)
2. coverage: rough share of the frame the biome's structures fill (0..1).
3. reads_as: what the background reads as in one phrase (e.g. "dark skyline of blocky towers", "flat floor, nothing behind").
4. scale: do the structures read as big (buildings, megastructure) or tiny (pebbles, specks)?
5. light: is the background lit enough to read (not black on black)?
6. verdict: pass (usable as a reel preview of that world) or fail, with the main reason.

# Rubric: Backdrop Plates behind the Robot Clips (round P1)

One composite per biome and Angle: the first beauty frame of the Angle's idle Clip (the Android, on duel Angles also the giant machine) laid over the Backdrop Plate rendered from the same locked Shot Camera. The plate holds the floor and a Geo-Scatter biome scaled into a megastructure behind the fighters. No Shadow Pass is applied in these composites.

Per shot, judge:
1. backdrop_visible: does the megastructure show behind the fighters? (yes / barely / no)
2. figure_reads: do the Android (and giant) stand out from the backdrop, silhouette and limbs readable? (yes / partly / no)
3. grounded: do the feet meet the plate's floor at a believable height, not floating above or sunk below a visible horizon? (yes / unclear / no)
4. scale: do the structures read as big (megastructure, buildings) rather than props or specks?
5. artefacts: a visible plane edge, a hard empty gap, a structure cut through the fighters, or anything that looks broken (name it, or "none").
6. verdict: pass (usable behind the fight in the show) or fail, with the main reason.

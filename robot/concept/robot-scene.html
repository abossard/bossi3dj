1 · Pipeline

Blender builds, Magic plays

Blender stops being the show renderer. It becomes the workshop that renders a Clip Library in high quality and writes one Render Package. Magic plays that package to the music. A small live Blender scene adds what has to react within a frame.

Blender · render framework

  • One Android, one rig, one set, many Clips.
  • Each Clip: one move from one camera, a whole number of beats long.
  • Passes per Clip: beauty, Android Matte, depth, object ids (fists, eyes, impact spots).
  • Renders in EEVEE or Cycles, off the show machine's clock.

Render Package

  • Clips as Hap files (all keyframes, decoded on the GPU, Magic's best case).
  • manifest.json: length in beats, Pose Key in and out, Cut Points, tier, camera.
  • The camera path and rig of each Clip, for the live scene.

Magic scene

  • Generated from the manifest with magicproj.py, never built by hand.
  • VideoFile modules feed an InputSelector; audio Globals choose the input on the beat.
  • Mask cuts the Live Layer to the Android or the room; Blend adds it.

Blender · live

  • Loads the same rig and camera path, set to the frame Magic reports.
  • Draws only thin layers on black: sparks, HUD, light flashes, particles.
  • Publishes its own Syphon server; Magic adds it, so no alpha channel is needed.

2 · Pre-rendered or live

Quality ahead of time, reaction in the frame

The planet taught the rule: a real-time scene on this GPU fights for every millisecond and still flickers. So everything heavy is rendered once, and live work is kept small.

Pre-rendered Clips

  • The Android, its materials, its moves: idles, strikes, blocks, hits, finishers.
  • The set and its lighting, with GI, reflections, motion blur and depth of field.
  • Camera moves inside a Clip: push-ins, orbits, low angles.
  • The Mattes: Android coverage, depth, ids of fists, eyes and impact spots.
  • Opponents, if any: drones, a rival, a giant machine.

Why: a Clip looks the same every night, at any quality the render farm can afford, at zero cost to the show GPU beyond decoding.

Live Layers

  • Sparks and debris at the real impact spot, on the snare.
  • HUD, target brackets and readouts that track the fists.
  • Light flashes, strobe, glitch, colour shifts on kick and drop.
  • Particles and smoke in the room, cut behind the Android by the Matte.

Why: it reacts within a frame and follows tempo changes a Clip cannot. It stays cheap because Blender draws thin layers on black, not a full scene.

3 · Audio to Clips

Clips land on the beat

Magic already decides what the music means. Here it picks the next Clip at the next Cut Point. Sixteen bars of a track, with the playhead running on this page's clock:

Whole beats

Clips are 4, 8 or 16 beats long. At 124 BPM, 4 beats are 1.94 s, 58 frames at 30 fps.

Tempo follows the music

VideoFile Speed = live BPM ÷ 124. Within about ±15 % the motion still reads; beyond that a second render tempo is needed.

Pose Keys join Clips

Every Clip starts and ends on a Pose Key: guard, stagger or crouch. A cut goes only into a Clip that starts on the key the current one reaches.

Intensity Tier

Magic's energy picks the tier: calm (idles, circling), fight (strikes, blocks), peak (combos, finishers). A drop forces a finisher at the next bar.

Live on every hit

Kick: a light flash in the Live Layer. Snare: sparks at the fist. These never wait for a cut.

4 · Hard parts

Where this can fail

Numbers marked "estimate" are not measured yet. Each gets a test before the library is rendered.

risk: high

Continuity between Clips

A cut from a punch to a kick shows a jump unless both share a Pose Key, the same camera position at the cut, and the same lighting. Plan: few Pose Keys, every Clip rendered from a held rest pose, a 2 to 4 frame blend on cuts within one camera, hard cuts only when the camera changes.

risk: high

Live Layers off the fist

Magic does not report a VideoFile's playhead. Magic has to count the Clip's time itself and send clip and frame to Blender; any drift puts sparks beside the fist. Plan: a sync Clip with a frame counter burnt in, locked-off or slow cameras in round 1, and Live Layers that tolerate a frame of error (glow, not pinpoint).

risk: medium

File size and decoding

Hap at 1080p30 is about 25 to 35 MB/s (estimate), Hap Q twice that. 80 Clips of 4 s make 8 to 11 GB, Mattes on top. Plan: Android Matte in the Hap alpha, depth and ids in a half-size second file, and one long file per tier with Start and End Time jumps instead of 80 open modules. Measure Magic's memory first.

risk: medium

The Android itself

A model, a rig and believable fight animation are the largest unknown. Hand-keyed fights eat weeks; motion capture libraries need licences and cleanup. Plan: decide the source first (decision 2 below), then prove one 8 beat combo end to end.

risk: medium

Render time and the GPU slot

About 7,000 frames for the first library. EEVEE at a few seconds a frame is hours; Cycles is overnight (estimate). Renders need the one Blender slot this machine has. Plan: render overnight, never while the planet sessions work.

risk: low

Magic wiring

Every piece exists in Magic: VideoFile, InputSelector, Mask, Blend, SyphonClient, audio Globals. Plan: a generator in robot/ writes the scene from the manifest; magicproj.py check and a Syphon grab prove it.

7 · Framework proposal

One pipeline, eight switches

H1 to H8 are modules of one build, not eight projects. Blender builds and renders once; a generator writes the Magic scene; Magic runs the show; live Blender layers are optional. Pick an idea to see which modules it switches on. The example lights H4 + H2 + H7 + H1.

Example in motion: the Clip Graph picks the next Clip on the bar (H4), Magic recolours the rim from Light Passes (H2), sparks and fog sit in depth at the Hits (H7), the world behind is live (H1).

Circle

Original twigl.app shader by @YoheiNishitsuji (https://x.com/YoheiNishitsuji?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor), ISF V2.0 Version by @dot2dot

Retains the folded torus distance and accumulated inverse-glow rendering; the thirty componentwise folds are evaluated analytically for show performance.

Pixel Tunnel

Original by @dot2dot (bareimage). ISF 2.0 Conversion by @dot2dot (bareimage)

Retains the mirrored cellular tunnel and its boxed voxel faces; MIDImix controls tunnel scale, wandering and the three painted channel colours.

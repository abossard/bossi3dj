Incoming OSC (what Magic sends)

Blender bridge

RTT

Send a message to Blender

numbers → float, 3i → int32, anything else → string. Sent to the Blender and Godot targets, bypassing the relay.

Sliders (hand-driven values)

One slider per row of the contract, in wire units (0..1, an integer for an index, a trigger's strength), starting at the wire default. A yellow name has moved off its default.

Generators (stand in for Magic)

addresswavefreq Hzrate Hzminmaxsent
A generator sends to the Blender and Godot targets directly. To test the relay path instead, send to the lab port with an external tool.
